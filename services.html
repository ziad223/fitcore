The full build package for offices, retail units, restaurants, villas and apartments. We take an empty or tired space and hand back a finished, usable interior — with the landlord and authority paperwork closed out.

Where the base build is already there, we work around live operations: night shifts, phased handovers, protected floors and daily clean-downs.

  • Demolition & strip-out
  • Gypsum partitions & ceilings
  • Glass partitions & doors
  • Flooring & skirting
  • Joinery installation
  • Final finishes & snagging

One contract from first sketch to final clean. You approve a concept, a material board and a price, and the same company that drew it builds it — so nothing is lost between the designer's intent and what the carpenter reads.

Best for clients who want a single number and a single person to hold responsible.

  • Space planning & layouts
  • 3D visuals & walkthroughs
  • Material & finish boards
  • Lighting & joinery detailing
  • IFC & shop drawings
  • Costing & value engineering

Made to your dimensions, not to a catalogue. Kitchens, wardrobes, dressing rooms, reception desks, wall panelling, vanities and loose furniture — cut and assembled in the workshop, then installed by the team that surveyed the site.

Materials range from laminate and veneer through to lacquer, fluted timber, stone tops and integrated lighting.

  • Kitchens & pantries
  • Wardrobes & dressing rooms
  • Reception & retail counters
  • Wall panelling & feature walls
  • Vanities & bathroom units
  • Loose & upholstered furniture

Electrical, plumbing, HVAC and low-current works planned alongside the fit-out programme so first fix is finished before ceilings close. Executed by licensed technicians and coordinated with the building's own consultants.

We prepare the drawings, submit for approval and arrange inspections and energisation.

  • Power & lighting distribution
  • DB upgrades & containment
  • Plumbing & drainage
  • HVAC ducting & grilles
  • Data, CCTV & access control
  • Testing, commissioning & certificates

Annual maintenance contracts for villas, offices, retail units and small portfolios — scheduled preventive visits plus a call-out number that gets answered. We keep a record of every asset we touch, so the next visit starts from what we already know.

One-off repairs and small works are welcome too; you do not need a contract to get help.

  • Preventive maintenance schedules
  • AC servicing & duct cleaning
  • Electrical & plumbing repairs
  • Handyman & small works
  • Emergency call-outs
  • Asset registers & reporting

The last layer, and the one everybody looks at. Proper preparation, correct primers and finish coats applied in controlled conditions — because a decorative wall is only as good as the substrate under it.

Includes specialist applied finishes and full floor replacement with subfloor levelling.

  • Decorative & standard painting
  • Microcement & venetian plaster
  • Wallpaper & fabric panels
  • Tiling & natural stone
  • Parquet, vinyl & carpet
  • Epoxy & industrial floors

How a job runs

Sheet M-01
Method statement
Five stages
01

Survey & brief

We measure the space, check the building's rules and services, and write down what you actually need.

02

Design & costing

Layouts, visuals and a material board, priced line by line. Nothing moves to site until you sign off both.

03

Approvals

Drawings submitted to the developer, landlord or authority, and the permits chased so your start date holds.

04

Build

One project manager, one site team, weekly progress photos, joinery running in parallel in the workshop.

05

Snag & handover

Joint walkthrough, snag list closed, warranties and as-builts issued, defects cover starts.
