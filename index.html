Who we are

One contractor. The whole interior.

Fitcore Technical Services is a Dubai-based fit-out and technical services contractor. We work with developers, landlords, retailers and homeowners who want a single team accountable for the drawing, the build and the day the doors open.

Our own joinery and site supervision means fewer hand-offs, tighter tolerances and a programme that holds. Every project runs on approved shop drawings, a fixed scope and a named project manager you can call.

How a job runs

Sheet M-01
Method statement
Five stages
01

Survey & brief

We measure the space, check the building's rules and services, and write down what you actually need. You get a scope you can read without a dictionary.

02

Design & costing

Layouts, 3D visuals and a material board, priced line by line. Nothing moves to site until you have signed off both the look and the number.

03

Approvals

We prepare and submit drawings to the developer, landlord or authority, and chase the permits so your programme starts on the date we promised.

04

Build

One project manager, one site team, weekly progress photos. Joinery is made in the workshop while first fix runs on site, so trades do not queue.

05

Snag & handover

We walk the space with you, close the snag list, hand over warranties and as-built drawings, and stay on call through the defects period.
